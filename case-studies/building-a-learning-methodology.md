# Building a Learning Methodology with AI

## Background

When I first started learning Data Analytics, I used AI like many beginners: asking questions and looking for explanations.

Over time, that interaction changed.

Instead of asking AI for answers, I began using it to challenge my reasoning, review my work, improve my communication, and document what I learned.

This document describes how that methodology emerged through months of real conversations and iterative learning.
## The Pattern That Emerged

After reviewing months of conversations, I noticed that my interactions with AI consistently followed the same pattern.

Instead of asking for direct solutions, I naturally developed an iterative learning process:

```text
Problem
    ↓
Independent reasoning
    ↓
Build a hypothesis
    ↓
Discuss with AI
    ↓
Validate assumptions
    ↓
Refine the solution
    ↓
Document the learning
    ↓
Create reusable assets
```

This pattern appeared repeatedly across technical learning, interview preparation, professional communication, portfolio development, and project documentation.

The consistency of this workflow eventually became the foundation of this repository.
